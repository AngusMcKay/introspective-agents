# Corrie C-Van 2.0 diesel (2021 to 2024): owner's workshop notes (fictional)

Wheel nuts must be tightened to 160 Nm in a diagonal sequence, using a calibrated torque wrench. Re-check the torque after the first 50 miles following any wheel removal.

Tyre pressures for normal loads are 3.4 bar front and 3.6 bar rear. At full payload the rear pressure increases to 4.1 bar.

The engine takes 6.2 litres of 5W-30 oil meeting the C2 specification, including the filter. The oil and filter should be changed every 12,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first.

The timing belt and water pump are replaced together every 100,000 miles or 8 years, whichever comes first.

The diesel particulate filter (DPF) regenerates automatically during sustained driving above 40 mph for around 15 minutes. A DPF warning light that stays on after such a drive should be checked by a garage.

The AdBlue tank holds 21 litres. A warning appears when the remaining range falls below 1,500 miles.

Front brake pads have a minimum thickness of 2 mm. The front discs have a minimum thickness of 26 mm, which is stamped on the disc hub.

The sump plug is tightened to 40 Nm with a new sealing washer every time it is removed.
