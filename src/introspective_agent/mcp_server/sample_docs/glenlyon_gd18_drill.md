# Glenlyon GD-18 cordless combi drill: instruction manual (fictional)

The GD-18 is an 18 V cordless combi drill with a 13 mm keyless chuck and a maximum torque of 55 Nm.

The clutch has 20 torque settings plus a drill setting. Use lower clutch settings for small screws and soft materials, and increase the setting until the screw seats flush without stripping.

Hammer mode is for masonry only and should be used with masonry bits. Do not use hammer mode on tiles until the glaze has been drilled through in normal drill mode.

Maximum drilling capacity is 13 mm in masonry, 13 mm in steel and 38 mm in wood.

The 2.0 Ah battery charges fully in 45 minutes with the supplied charger. Store batteries at around 40 percent charge if the drill will not be used for more than a month.

Before drilling into walls, check for hidden cables and pipes with a detector. Cables are commonly run vertically and horizontally from sockets and switches.
